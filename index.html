Eddie's Couch

Three people. One big house. Nine couches.

Full screen on iPhone: tap Share → Add to Home Screen, then play from the new icon. No browser bars.

How to play

Theo & Zach: the mess squad

  • Mess up every seat of all 9 couches. Theo hurls. Zach flings saucy nuggets.
  • Hold to charge a MEGA shot. It flies farther and soaks several seats.
  • Eat nuggets, drumsticks and mac & cheese to refill. Eddie's fridge has a whole chicken.
  • Eddie scrubs half-messed couches. Finish a couch and it's ruined forever.
  • Missed shots make puddles. Eddie slips on them.
  • Theo can hide under the dining table or the bed. Zach can flex out of one grab per life.
  • Zach's gut fills up as he eats. A full gut is his power-up: hold to turn around and let it rip. Then get to a bidet before the timer runs out. Every toilet has one.
  • Get caught 3 times and it's time-out for good.

Eddie: the clean team

  • Keep the house clean until bedtime (2½ minutes). Lose 5 couches and it's over. A fully stained couch takes 10 seconds to soak through: scrub one seat to save it.
  • Grab Theo or Zach to send them to time-out. Lunge to close the gap.
  • Hold GRAB next to a stain to scrub it, or next to a puddle to mop it.
  • YELL freezes everyone nearby for a moment.

Keyboard + mouse

  • W A S D move · Mouse look · Shift sprint
  • Click / Space hurl / fling / grab (hold for mega or scrub)
  • Q / Right-click throw toy · yell
  • 1 2 3 pick a character · M mute · Esc pause

Touch

  • Left thumb: drag to move (push all the way to sprint). You aim where you face.
  • Right thumb: drag to look
  • Big button: tap to hurl / fling / grab, hold for mega or scrub
  • Small button: throw toy · yell
